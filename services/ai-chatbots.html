The Implementation Process

How we build an AI that perfectly represents your brand.

1. Knowledge Extraction

We securely ingest your website, PDFs, internal FAQs, and past help desk tickets into a vector database to build the bot's comprehensive "brain."

2. Persona & Guardrails

We meticulously engineer the system prompt so the bot adopts your exact brand tone and strictly refuses to answer out-of-scope questions.

3. Integration & Handoff

We connect the bot to your website, WhatsApp, or Instagram DMs, and build seamless "human-handoff" protocols for complex support queries.

4. Ongoing Refinement

We continuously monitor conversation logs, correct any hallucinations, and add new knowledge to improve accuracy over time.
