The Buyer Intent Framework

We don't bid on broad terms that waste money. We target users at the exact moment they are ready to purchase.

Top of Funnel (We Avoid)

Information Gatherers

"How to fix a leaky pipe"

High search volume, but extremely low conversion rate. Amateurs waste budget here.
Middle of Funnel

Comparison Shoppers

"Best plumbers in Chicago"

Good intent, evaluating options. We capture these with strong value propositions and social proof.
Bottom of Funnel (Our Target)

Ready to Buy

"Emergency 24/7 plumber near me"

Highest conversion rate. We aggressively bid here to ensure you win the click and the customer.

Our Campaign Methodology

How we build campaigns that outperform the competition.

1

Tracking Infrastructure

Before spending a dime, we implement robust server-side conversion tracking (GTM, GA4) so every lead is perfectly attributed to the exact keyword.

2

Architectural Build-out

We structure SKAGs (Single Keyword Ad Groups) or STAGs (Single Theme Ad Groups) ensuring your ad copy matches the user's exact search intent.

3

Landing Page Synergy

We don't send traffic to your homepage. We pair ads with highly-relevant, conversion-optimized landing pages designed specifically for that campaign.

4

Aggressive Optimization

Once live, we monitor search term reports daily, adding negative keywords to block bad traffic, and adjusting bids to maximize impression share on winners.
